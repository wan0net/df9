The project

Spacebase, carefully reconstructed.

This independent, non-commercial project recreates Spacebase DF-9 with TypeScript, Three.js, and the original game's published Lua source as its behavioral reference. Graphics, layout, simulation rules, AI, construction, hazards, research, and events are cross-checked against the original.
